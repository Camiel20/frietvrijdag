HOW DOES IT WORK?

Ordering fries on Friday

Everyone picks their own fries using their own link. A wheel decides who pays the whole bill. Everyone else pays that person back through a payment link.

🔗

1. Your own link

You get one personal link. With it, the app knows it's you; no need to log in anywhere.

Don't share your link. Whoever has your link orders as you.

The app opens in English when you use your English link. You can switch language any time with the NL / EN button at the top right.

📱 Tip: add it to your home screen.
iPhone (Safari): share button ⬆︎ → Add to Home Screen.
Android (Chrome): menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
🍟

2. Open the order

Fancy fries? Tap Open the order. There is only one order per Friday: if it's already open, you simply join in.

On any other day than Friday, you open the one for the coming Friday in advance.

🛒

3. Choose what you want

  • Search or tap a category, then tap + next to what you want.
  • Special requests (mayo on the side, no onion…) go in the note field on that line.
  • Everything is saved automatically. You can change it until the wheel has been spun.

The menu is in Dutch, just like on the De Posthoorn website. Prices come from their website; bottle/can deposit (statiegeld) is included.

📖 A few menu words: friet = fries · met = with · zonder = without (friet zonder = plain fries) · ui = onion · friet speciaal = with mayo, curry ketchup and onion · friet oorlog = with mayo, peanut sauce and onion · kroket = croquette · frikandel = Dutch minced-meat sausage · broodje = bread roll / sandwich · sauzen = sauces · dranken = drinks · ijsjes = ice cream
🎡

4. The wheel: who pays?

Everyone who ordered something is on the wheel. Is everyone ready? Then someone who ordered taps Spin the wheel.

  • From that moment the order is closed: nobody can change anything.
  • The wheel is spun only once. Everyone sees it spin, even if you open the page later.
So only spin once everyone has ordered!
💳

5. Are you paying?

  • You see the complete order list; with Copy you can paste it straight into the order at De Posthoorn.
  • Create a Tikkie (or another payment request) without a fixed amount and paste the link into the app → Save. Everyone sees in the app exactly how much they owe you.
  • Under Who has paid? you see who says they've paid. Is the money in your account? Then tap Received.
  • Not everyone paid yet? Copy reminder gives you a message for the group chat.
  • Optional: take a photo of the receipt under Read and check the receipt. The app then tells you where the receipt differs from what was ordered.
✅

6. Paying back

At the top it says "You pay … € …". Tap Pay via … link, pay that amount and then tap I have paid in the app.

Once it's in, the payer ticks it off. If something from an earlier Friday is still open, you'll see it at the top of your page.
